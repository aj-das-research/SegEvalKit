\section{Reported vs.\ reproduced}
\label{sec:reported}

R-Super reports patient-level tumour detection on the PanTS test set: a patient is called positive when the
predicted tumour volume exceeds a threshold. We reproduce this twice (\cref{tab:reported}): \textbf{(A)} with their own
code path, i.e.\ their \texttt{detection()} volume (1\,mm resampling and a 1-erode / 2-dilate opening), their
ground truth (\texttt{number of pancreatic lesion instances}\,$\ge$\,1 in \texttt{metadata\_pants.csv}) and their
threshold grid; and \textbf{(B)} with the raw predicted volume and the reference masks of SegEvalKit. In both
we pick the threshold whose specificity is closest to their 91\%; brackets are 95\% bootstrap intervals over patients,
with the threshold re-chosen in each resample. The two ground truths agree on \nGTagree{} of \nCases{} patients.

\textbf{Good.} Mean Dice is $\ge$\,0.85 on \mfNOrgansAbove{} of \mfNOrgans{} organs and vessels (pancreas
\mfPancDice, median \mfPancDiceMed; liver \mfLiverDice). Paired with TotalSegmentator on the same patients, MedFormer
has the higher Dice in \fracDiceMin--\fracDiceMax\% of patients on every shared organ, largest on the duodenum.
\textbf{Weak.} The common bile duct (\mfCBDDice) and the gallbladder (\mfGallDice{} macro, \mfGallPooled{} pooled)
are the weakest structures. Mean HD95 is far above the median (spleen \mfSpleenHD{} vs.\ \mfSpleenHDMed\,mm): the
\tailSpleenN{} spleen cases above 50\,mm make up \tailSpleenShare\% of the mean. \tailSpleenRefEmpty{} of them are
scans with an \emph{empty} reference spleen mask on which MedFormer still predicts a spleen (\refEmptySpleen{}
such scans; TotalSegmentator predicts one in \tsPredOnEmptySpleen), and the other \tailSpleenBoth{} are good
segmentations (median Dice \tailSpleenBothDice) with stray voxels far from the organ. Excluding empty references,
mean spleen HD95 is \mfSpleenHDnonEmpty\,mm. The left kidney shows the same pattern (\tailKidLN{} cases,
\tailKidLRefEmpty{} with an empty reference). This tail is why MedFormer's mean HD95 is worse than TotalSegmentator's
on \nHDmeanWorse{} of ten organs although its HD95 is lower in most patients on \nHDfracBetter{} of them, and why
the two ranking schemes disagree on the \rankFlips: ranking by mean Dice puts TotalSegmentator first, ranking per
patient puts MedFormer first (bootstrap Kendall's $\tau \ge$ \tauMin{} for both schemes).

\medskip
MedFormer finds \nRefDetected{} of \nRefLesions{} tumours: \detBig\% of those of 1\,mL or more, but only
\detSmall\% of the \nSmall{} below 0.5\,mL. Only \lesPrec{} of its predicted tumours are real
(\fpScan{} false tumours per scan); most false alarms are in tumour-free patients (\fpNeg{} per scan vs.\ \fpPos{} in
tumour patients) and are small (median \fpNegMedML\,mL), so in tumour patients the per-patient lesion precision is
\posPrec. When a tumour is present, its voxel Dice is \posDice{} on average (median \posDiceMed). The lesion
probabilities are well calibrated in the band around the masks.

\begin{figure}[!h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.84\linewidth]{figures/f_froc_pr.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Lesion-level detection curves}, ranking each predicted tumour by its maximum probability: FROC with the
CPM (left) and precision--recall with the average precision (right), over all \nRefLesions{} reference tumours.}

\noindent The FROC score (CPM) is \cpm{}: sensitivity is \frocEighth{} at 1/8 and \frocOne{} at one false tumour per
scan, and flat beyond, because the tumours still missed are never predicted at any threshold; the lesion-level average
precision is \lesAP{}. \emph{Localized} patient-level detection counts a flagged patient only if a predicted tumour
overlaps the reference tumour: it lowers the sensitivity at specificity 0.90 from \sensPlain{} to \sensLoc{} and the
AUC from \aucPlain{} to \aucLoc{}, so some patients are flagged for a false tumour in the wrong place.

\section{Metric definitions}
$P$ prediction, $G$ reference, $TP/FP/FN$ voxel counts; $D_{P\to G}$ distances (mm) from each surface voxel of $P$ to
the surface of $G$; $\partial P$ surface of $P$; ``les'' marks counts of tumour blobs (a reference tumour is found
when a predicted blob overlaps it).

{\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l X c@{}}
\toprule
Metric & Formula & Meaning & Better \\
\midrule
Dice & $2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)$ & Overlap of prediction and reference & $\uparrow$ \\
NSD$_\tau$ & $\big(|D_{P\to G}\!\le\!\tau|+|D_{G\to P}\!\le\!\tau|\big)/(|\partial P|+|\partial G|)$ & Fraction of both
  surfaces within $\tau$\,=\,\nsdTol\,mm of the other & $\uparrow$ \\
HD95 & $\max\big(P_{95}(D_{P\to G}),P_{95}(D_{G\to P})\big)$ & Boundary error that 95\% of surface points stay within & $\downarrow$ \\
Pooled Dice & $2\sum_i TP_i/(2\sum_i TP_i+\sum_i FP_i+\sum_i FN_i)$ & Dice over all voxels of all cases (large
  structures weigh more) & $\uparrow$ \\
Tumours found & $TP_{\mathrm{les}}/(TP_{\mathrm{les}}+FN_{\mathrm{les}})$ & Fraction of reference tumours overlapped
  by a prediction & $\uparrow$ \\
Predicted tumours real & $TP_{\mathrm{les}}/(TP_{\mathrm{les}}+FP_{\mathrm{les}})$ & Fraction of predicted tumours that
  overlap a reference tumour & $\uparrow$ \\
False tumours / scan & $\sum FP_{\mathrm{les}}/n_{\mathrm{scans}}$ & Predicted tumours overlapping no reference, per scan & $\downarrow$ \\
Patient sens.\ / spec. & $TP/(TP+FN)$, $TN/(TN+FP)$ over patients & Tumour patients flagged / tumour-free patients not
  flagged & $\uparrow$ \\
Patient F1 & $2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)$ over patients & Balance of patient-level sensitivity and precision & $\uparrow$ \\
AUC & $\int \mathrm{TPR}\,d\,\mathrm{FPR}$ & Probability that a tumour patient gets a larger predicted volume than a
  tumour-free one & $\uparrow$ \\
Localized sensitivity & $TP_{\mathrm{loc}}/(TP+FN)$ over patients & A flagged tumour patient counts only if a predicted
  tumour overlaps the reference tumour & $\uparrow$ \\
CPM (FROC) & $\frac{1}{7}\sum_{r\in\{\frac18,\dots,8\}} \mathrm{sens}(r)$ & Mean tumour sensitivity at 1/8 to 8 false tumours
  per scan (LUNA16) & $\uparrow$ \\
Lesion AP & $\sum_k (R_k-R_{k-1})\,P_k$ & Area under the tumour-level precision--recall curve, tumours ranked by
  probability & $\uparrow$ \\
ECE & $\sum_b \frac{|B_b|}{N}|\mathrm{acc}(B_b)-\mathrm{conf}(B_b)|$ & Gap between confidence and accuracy (15 bins) & $\downarrow$ \\
Brier & $\frac1N\sum_i (p_i-g_i)^2$ & Mean squared error of the voxel probabilities & $\downarrow$ \\
\bottomrule

\section{Limitations and reproduction}
\begin{itemize}
\item PanTS publishes no evaluation script for organ metrics; NSD tolerance, detection rule and empty-mask policy are
  SegEvalKit's documented defaults, so organ and lesion-level metrics are not leaderboard numbers; the patient-level comparison of \cref{sec:reported} uses R-Super's own code, matched on specificity because the published threshold is not stated.
\item Some reference masks are empty for organs present in the scan field (e.g.\ \refEmptySpleen{} spleens); a
  prediction there counts as a large error.
\item Only the mask-trained checkpoint was evaluated, with one disclosed fix to the official inference code.
\end{itemize}
