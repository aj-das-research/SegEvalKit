    Full metric catalogue#

    SegEvalKit registers 56 metrics in 7 families. This page is generated from the metric registry at build time, so it always matches the installed code. Click a name for the full definition.

    $ segevalkit metrics -v      # the same list on the command line

    Overlap (voxel counting)#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Dice similarity coefficient
    dice    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Twice the overlap divided by the total size of both masks; the most common segmentation score.
    Intersection over union (Jaccard index)
    iou    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Overlap divided by the union of both masks; a stricter monotone transform of Dice.
    Volumetric overlap error
    voe    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – – One minus IoU: the fraction of the union that is not shared (used in LiTS / SLIVER07).
    Precision (positive predictive value)
    precision    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of predicted voxels that are truly foreground; penalises over-segmentation.
    Recall (sensitivity, true-positive rate)
    recall    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of the reference that was found; penalises under-segmentation.
    Specificity (true-negative rate)
    specificity    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of background left as background; near 1 for almost any 3D prediction because background dominates.
    False-positive rate (fall-out)
    fpr    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – – Fraction of background wrongly labelled foreground (1 − specificity).
    False-negative rate (miss rate)
    fnr    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – – Fraction of the reference that was missed (1 − recall).
    F-beta score
    fbeta    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Weighted harmonic mean of precision and recall; beta > 1 favours recall (missing tissue is worse).
    Tversky index
    tversky    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Asymmetric Dice that weights false positives (alpha) and false negatives (beta) differently.
    Voxel accuracy
    accuracy    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of all voxels labelled correctly; uninformative in 3D because background dominates.
    Balanced accuracy
    balanced_accuracy    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Mean of sensitivity and specificity; equals the ROC AUC of a binary prediction.

    Volume agreement#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Predicted volume
    pred_volume    		 descriptive [0, ∞) mL spacing Physical volume of the prediction (descriptive, not a score).
    Reference volume
    ref_volume    		 descriptive [0, ∞) mL spacing Physical volume of the reference (descriptive; the usual stratification variable).
    Signed volume difference
    volume_difference    		 → 0 (−∞, ∞) mL spacing Predicted minus reference volume in mL; positive means over-segmentation (bias).
    Absolute volume difference
    absolute_volume_difference    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mL spacing Magnitude of the volume error in mL (used e.g. by ISLES and autoPET).
    Relative volume difference
    relative_volume_difference    		 → 0 [−1, ∞) – – Signed volume error relative to the reference volume; −0.2 means 20 % under-segmented.
    Volumetric similarity
    volumetric_similarity    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – One minus the absolute volume difference normalised by the summed volumes; ignores location.

    Boundary & surface distance#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Hausdorff distance
    hd    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing The single worst boundary error: the largest distance from any surface point to the other surface.
    Percentile Hausdorff distance
    hd_percentile    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing Hausdorff distance with the most extreme (100−q) % of boundary points ignored, for robustness to outliers.
    95th-percentile Hausdorff distance
    hd95    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing Near-worst boundary error, ignoring the 5 % most extreme surface points (BraTS, KiTS, TopCoW...).
    Average symmetric surface distance
    assd    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing Mean distance over all surface points of both masks: the typical boundary error in mm.
    Mean average surface distance
    masd    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing Average of the two directed mean distances; unlike ASSD, both surfaces weigh equally regardless of size.
    Normalized surface Dice
    nsd    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – spacing Fraction of both surfaces lying within a clinically acceptable tolerance tau of the other surface.
    Boundary IoU
    boundary_iou    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – spacing IoU restricted to a thin band inside each boundary; sensitive to boundary quality even for large objects.
    Centroid distance
    centroid_distance    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing Distance between the centres of mass of the two masks; a localisation error insensitive to shape.

    Topology & centreline#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Betti-0 error (components)
    betti0_error    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Difference in the number of connected pieces: counts spurious islands and fragmentation.
    Betti-1 error (tunnels/loops)
    betti1_error    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Difference in the number of loops/handles: e.g. a vessel ring (circle of Willis) opened or falsely closed.
    Betti-2 error (cavities)
    betti2_error    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Difference in the number of enclosed holes: e.g. a filled-in lumen or a spurious internal void.
    Euler characteristic error
    euler_error    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Difference of the Euler characteristic (components − loops + cavities); one number for overall topology.
    Centreline Dice
    cldice    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Dice computed on skeletons: rewards connected, complete tubular structures (vessels, airways, ducts).

    Detection & instance (lesion-wise)#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Lesion-wise recall (detection sensitivity)
    lesion_recall    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of reference lesions that were detected, regardless of their size.
    Lesion-wise precision
    lesion_precision    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Fraction of predicted lesions that correspond to a real lesion (1 − false-discovery rate).
    Lesion-wise F1 score
    lesion_f1    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Harmonic mean of lesion precision and recall: one number for 'found the lesions, and only them'.
    Absolute lesion count difference
    lesion_count_difference    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – How many more or fewer lesions were predicted than exist (ISLES'22 secondary metric).
    False-positive lesion count
    false_positive_lesions    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Number of predicted components that overlap no reference lesion.
    Missed lesion count
    false_negative_lesions    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Number of reference lesions that were not detected.
    Split lesions
    split_count    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Number of reference lesions covered by more than one predicted component (over-fragmentation).
    Merged lesions
    merge_count    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – – Number of predicted components that cover more than one reference lesion (under-separation).
    Panoptic quality
    panoptic_quality    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Detection quality × segmentation quality of matched lesions: F1 of IoU>0.5 matches times their mean IoU.
    Lesion-wise Dice (BraTS 2023)
    lesionwise_dice    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Dice averaged over lesions, with missed and spurious lesions each scoring 0; small lesions count as much as large.
    Lesion-wise HD95 (BraTS 2023)
    lesionwise_hd95    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) mm spacing HD95 averaged over lesions; a missed or a spurious lesion scores the empty-mask distance penalty.
    Lesion-wise normalized surface Dice
    lesionwise_nsd    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – spacing NSD averaged over lesions; a missed or a spurious lesion scores 0, so small lesions count as much as large.
    Lesion-level average precision
    lesion_ap    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – – Area under the lesion precision-recall curve obtained by ranking predicted lesions by confidence.

    Probabilistic & calibration#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Soft Dice
    soft_dice    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – probabilities Dice computed with probabilities instead of a thresholded mask; rewards confident correct voxels.
    Area under the ROC curve
    auroc    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – probabilities Probability that a random foreground voxel scores higher than a random background voxel.
    Area under the precision-recall curve (average precision)
    auprc    		 ↑ higher [0, 1] – probabilities Average precision over all thresholds; unlike AUROC it stays informative under heavy class imbalance.
    Brier score
    brier    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – probabilities Mean squared difference between predicted probability and the true label; mixes calibration and sharpness.
    Negative log-likelihood
    nll    		 ↓ lower [0, ∞) – probabilities Average surprise of the true label under the predicted probabilities; punishes confident mistakes hard.
    Expected calibration error
    ece    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – probabilities Average gap between confidence and accuracy across confidence bins; 0 means perfectly calibrated.

    Agreement & information theory#

    Metric and key Better Range Unit Needs In one sentence
    Mutual information
    mutual_information    		 ↑ higher [0, 0.693147] nats – How much knowing the prediction reduces uncertainty about the reference label of a voxel.
    Variation of information
    variation_of_information    		 ↓ lower [0, 1.38629] nats – Information lost and gained between the two partitions; a true metric on partitions.
    Adjusted Rand index
    adjusted_rand_index    		 ↑ higher [−1, 1] – – Pairwise agreement of voxel labels, corrected for chance.
    Global consistency error
    global_consistency_error    		 ↓ lower [0, 1] – – Degree to which one segmentation is a refinement of the other; 0 when identical.
    Matthews correlation coefficient
    mcc    		 ↑ higher [−1, 1] – – Correlation between prediction and reference over all voxels; robust to class imbalance among TN-aware metrics.
    Cohen's kappa
    cohen_kappa    		 ↑ higher [−1, 1] – – Voxel agreement corrected for the agreement expected by chance.

    Metric sets#

    Named bundles accepted anywhere a metric list is expected (metrics=["default", "cldice"], --metrics distance,detection).

    Set Metrics
    default dice, iou, nsd, hd95, assd, precision, recall, relative_volume_difference
    overlap dice, iou, precision, recall, specificity, voe, fbeta, tversky, mcc, cohen_kappa, balanced_accuracy
    distance hd, hd95, assd, masd, nsd, boundary_iou
    volume pred_volume, ref_volume, absolute_volume_difference, relative_volume_difference, volumetric_similarity
    topology cldice, betti0_error, betti1_error, betti2_error, euler_error
    detection lesion_precision, lesion_recall, lesion_f1, panoptic_quality, lesionwise_dice, lesionwise_hd95, lesionwise_nsd, lesion_ap, lesion_count_difference, false_positive_lesions, false_negative_lesions, split_count, merge_count
    calibration ece, brier, nll, auroc, auprc, soft_dice
    agreement cohen_kappa, mcc, adjusted_rand_index, variation_of_information, mutual_information, global_consistency_error
    all every registered metric
    all_binary every metric that does not need probabilities
