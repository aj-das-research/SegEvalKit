# Research

SegEvalKit is grounded in two structured literature studies carried out for the project. They are reproduced
here in full, with every reference, so that each design decision in the library can be traced back to its source.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-variant:{ .lg } **[Metrics literature review](metrics-literature-review.md)**

    45 segmentation metrics in 8 families: verified equations, plain-language explanations, pitfalls,
    empty-mask conventions across libraries, a selection guide, and a survey of existing tools.

-   :material-database-search:{ .lg } **[Datasets & benchmarks survey](datasets-survey.md)**

    Public volumetric CT/MR segmentation datasets and challenges: modality, anatomy, label conventions,
    layouts, licences, download routes and their *official* evaluation protocols.

</div>
